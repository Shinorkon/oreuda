## Facet: Solo Leveling Lore & System Mechanics

**Research Date**: 2025
**Sources**: 14+ independent web searches across Solo Leveling Wiki, GameRant, CBR, analysis articles, manhwa lore sites
**Coverage**: System mechanics, visual language, ranks, dungeons, shadow army, titles, store, quests, stats, key phrases

---

### Key Findings

- The System is a magical program created by **Kandiaru** (the Architect) to find and prepare a human vessel for the **Shadow Monarch Ashborn** [^23^][^123^]. It chose Sung Jin-woo as its sole Player after the Double Dungeon incident [^26^].
- The System has a **level cap of 100** in the original programme, after which Ashborn's power transfer was designed to occur [^26^][^124^].
- The System's interface is **game-like with a blue holographic screen** that appears anywhere and anytime to notify Jinwoo [^124^][^128^].
- Time appears to **pause when Jin-woo interacts with the System window** — from his perspective he can review his interface without losing a second in the outside world [^26^].
- Each level-up grants **+1 to all stats automatically** and **5 stat points** to distribute manually. Daily quest completion grants **+3 additional stat points** [^23^][^25^].
- The Store uses **gold currency** earned from kills and quest completions, offering consumables, equipment, weapons, and two types of loot boxes: Blessed Random Box (gives what player wants) and Cursed Random Box (gives what player needs) [^23^][^26^].
- Jin-woo is the **only hunter who can level up** — all other hunters are locked at their awakening rank forever, unless they receive an extremely rare "second awakening" [^24^].
- Shadow soldiers are ranked in **7 grades** from Normal (weakest, E-C rank equivalent) to Grand Marshal (strongest, only Bellion holds this rank) [^62^][^68^].
- The Penalty Zone is a **desert world with no wind, sun, moon, or stars**, inhabited by Poison-fanged Giant Sand Centipedes, where Jinwoo must survive for 4 hours [^103^].
- The **Demon Castle** is a 100-floor S-Rank Instant Dungeon with bosses on floors 1 (Cerberus), 50 (Vulcan), 75 (Metus), and 100 (Baran) [^64^].
- The **command word "Arise"** is the activation phrase for Shadow Extraction — Jinwoo's most iconic line and the namesake of the game/app [^101^][^106^].
- The System was deliberately designed with a **video game interface** so Jinwoo could understand it without confusion, based on the demon race's ability to grow stronger by consuming souls [^23^][^26^].
- After the Monarchs War, Jinwoo **modified the System** and passed a version to his son Sung Su-ho in *Solo Leveling: Ragnarok*, which adapted itself with new features like pets and companions [^23^].

### System Features Inventory

#### 1. Daily Quests
- **Official name**: "The Preparation To Become Powerful" [^135^]
- **Tasks**: 100 Push-Ups, 100 Sit-Ups, 100 Squats, 10 Kilometer Run
- **Time limit**: 24 hours
- **Rewards**: Status Recovery (heals all injuries/fatigue), +3 Stat Points, Random Loot Box [^135^]
- **Penalty for failure**: Teleportation to the Penalty Zone for Survival quest [^25^][^31^]
- Daily quests pop up every morning automatically [^31^]

#### 2. Penalty Quests / Penalty Zone
- **Trigger**: Failing to complete the daily quest within 24 hours [^25^]
- **Quest name**: "Survival" [^102^]
- **Environment**: A vast desert where endless fields of sand stretch to the horizon. No wind, sun, moon, or stars. An empty sky like black ink spilled across a canvas. Despite lack of light source, you can clearly see surroundings [^103^]
- **Duration**: 4 hours of survival required [^102^][^31^]
- **Monsters**: Poison-fanged Giant Sand Centipedes — colossal vicious centipedes [^31^][^103^]
- **Difficulty scaling**: If Jinwoo attempts to hunt in the Penalty Zone, the difficulty readjusts/increases [^103^]
- **Exit condition**: Survive for 4 hours, then automatically teleported back [^31^]

#### 3. Main Quests
- Drive the central story forward [^26^]
- Send Jinwoo to specific dungeons tied to the succession plan
- **Examples**: Job Change dungeon, Demon Castle, return to Cartenon Temple [^26^]
- These quests have **no failure option** — completion is mandatory [^26^]
- Massive rewards upon completion

#### 4. Urgent Quests / Emergency Quests
- Triggered in critical situations, forcing Jinwoo to fight [^25^][^31^]
- Most common type: "Kill the Enemies" / "Defeat the Enemies"
- **Trigger condition**: At least one person near Jinwoo harbors deep killing intent toward him [^31^]
- **Reward**: Status Recovery
- **Penalty**: Death (by heart stopping) [^31^]
- Can be triggered inside dungeons AND in the real world
- The System detected Goto Ryuji's killing intent during their sparring match and alerted Jinwoo [^23^]

#### 5. Job Change Quest
- Received after crossing level 40 [^125^]
- Takes place in an **Instant Dungeon** with an army of armored soldiers
- Final boss: **Igris, the Blood-Red Commander** who guards an empty throne [^122^]
- After defeating Igris, must survive waves of enemies for as long as possible
- Initial class offered: **Necromancer** (hidden mage class)
- Because Jinwoo accumulated massive points, the class promoted to **Shadow Monarch** [^122^][^125^]
- Grants job-specific skills: Shadow Extraction, Shadow Preservation [^122^]
- Also grants Title: "The one who overcame adversity" [^122^]

#### 6. Side Quests
- Optional but reward rare items and significant stat boosts [^26^]
- Appear based on situations encountered naturally
- Example: "Courage of the Weak" — completed in the Double Dungeon by sacrificing himself [^31^]

#### 7. Stats Screen / Status Window
- Displays: Name, Job/Class, Title, Level, HP, MP, all stats, available ability points [^146^]
- Jinwoo can invest stat points with a "+" icon next to each stat [^104^]
- After distributing points, a "Distribute Points" confirmation appears [^104^]

#### 8. Inventory System
- **Unlimited storage** accessible at any time [^26^][^128^]
- Items can be **materialised and de-materialised instantly** [^26^]
- Jinwoo stores items in his shadow and can extract any number for use at any point [^134^]
- Effectively functions as pocket-dimensional storage within his shadow [^68^]

#### 9. Store / Shop
- **Digital marketplace** accessible within the System [^23^]
- Currency: **Gold** — received when Player kills monsters, completes quests, or sells items [^23^]
- Store inventory **scales to Player's current level** [^23^]
- **Categories**: Consumables (replenish health, mana, stamina), equipment, weapons [^23^]
- Notable item example: **Knight Killer dagger** [^23^]
- **Blessed Random Box**: Obtain an item the Player *wants* [^23^]
- **Cursed Random Box**: Obtain an item the Player *needs* (regardless of desire) [^23^][^26^]
- Purchase limits on consumables per day [^104^]

#### 10. Skills System
- Skills unlocked through quests, level-ups, and boss defeats [^26^]
- Each skill is **permanently retained once earned** [^26^]
- Skills can evolve into stronger versions (e.g., Sprint → Quicksilver, Vital Strike → Mutilate, Dagger Throw → Dagger Rush) [^67^]
- Rune Stones grant additional skills (e.g., Dominator's Touch evolved into Ruler's Authority) [^122^]

#### 11. Enemy Detection / Murderous Intent Warning
- System detects "murderous intent" from those who wish to harm Jinwoo [^124^][^128^]
- Warns Jinwoo of the slightest murderous intent in his vicinity [^23^]
- Automatically activates passive skills when Jinwoo is in danger [^26^]
- Example: During sparring with Goto Ryuji, System detected killing intent and alerted Jinwoo before any visible move [^23^]

### Stat Categories (Complete)

The System tracks five core statistics [^23^]:

| Stat | Full Name | Effect |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **STR** | Strength | Increases strength, speed, and attack power [^23^] |
| **AGI** | Agility | Increases dynamic vision, physical reaction speed, and evasion rate [^23^] |
| **STA/VIT** | Stamina/Vitality | Increases maximum HP and recovery speed [^23^] |
| **INT** | Intelligence | Increases maximum MP and MP recovery speed [^23^] |
| **SEN/PER** | Sense/Perception | Sharpens the five senses and increases danger perception ability [^23^] |

**Additional context**:
- Strength drives physical attack damage [^29^]
- Agility increases critical hit chance and critical damage [^29^]
- Intelligence reduces skill cooldown times and scales magic damage [^29^]
- Vitality increases HP and Defense [^29^]
- Perception increases damage cap (determines how much damage can be dealt) [^29^]

**Leveling rewards**:
- Every level-up: All stats +1 automatically, +5 stat points to distribute [^23^]
- Daily quest completion: +3 stat points [^23^]
- No per-stat cap — player can dump all points into one stat [^105^]
- Points can be reset and redistributed (for a cost in the game) [^107^]

### Hunter Ranks & Power System

#### The Six Official Ranks
| Rank | Description | Population |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **E-Rank** | Weakest hunters, barely above normal humans. "World's Weakest Hunter" was Jinwoo's starting label. Cleanup work, subsistence wages [^21^][^24^] | Most common |
| **D-Rank** | Working-class hunters, can handle E-D Gates. Stable income begins here [^21^] | Common |
| **C-Rank** | Solid middle class, standard dungeons, most professional hunters [^21^] | Less common |
| **B-Rank** | Recognizable hunters, can handle magic monsters, lead small raids [^21^] | Skilled minority |
| **A-Rank** | Elite tier, can solo dangerous raids, celebrity status, hedge-fund wealth [^21^] | Rare elite |
| **S-Rank** | Top officially recognized tier. Can clear A-Rank gates solo. National strategic assets [^21^][^24^] | Very rare (20-30 globally) |

#### Beyond S-Rank
- **National-Level Hunter**: Unofficial designation for S-Rank hunters whose power exceeds standard measurements to a national security degree. Only 5-7 exist globally at any time [^21^][^1^]
- Notable National-Level Hunters: Liu Zhigang (China), Thomas Andre (US), Christopher Reed (US), Goto Ryuji (Japan) [^21^]
- **False Rankers**: Hunters who can consciously control mana output, appearing ranked lower than they should be. Usually psychopaths who kill for fun [^24^]

#### Rank Properties
- Ranks are **normally fixed** from awakening — only "second awakening" (extremely rare) can change them [^24^]
- Jinwoo is the **only known exception** — his System allows continuous growth [^24^]
- China uses a 1-5 Star Rating instead of alphabetical [^24^]
- Power gaps between ranks are enormous — B-Ranks easily overpower C-Ranks, S-Ranks are exponentially above A-Ranks [^24^]
- Rank determines everything: income, reputation, dungeon access [^24^]

### Dungeon Types

| Type | Description | Key Example |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Normal Dungeon** | Standard gate, ranked E-S. Contains monsters + boss. Boss kill closes gate. 7-day clear limit or Dungeon Break occurs [^5^][^66^] | D-rank gate in Chapter 1 |
| **Red Gate** | Rare variant (B-Rank+). Seals shut on entry — no exit until boss dies or break occurs. **Time dilation**: 1 hour outside = 1 day inside. Hostile environments (frozen wastelands, etc.) [^5^][^63^] | White Tiger Guild Red Gate |
| **Double Dungeon** | Hidden dungeon inside a normal gate. Own rules/trial. Threat level disconnected from host gate rank [^5^][^66^] | Cartenon Temple |
| **Instant Dungeon** | Private dungeon only Jinwoo can access, issued by The System. Invisible to others [^5^][^66^] | Job Change dungeon |
| **Demon Castle** | 100-floor S-Rank Instant Dungeon. 4 major bosses on floors 1, 50, 75, 100 [^64^] | Demon Castle Arc |
| **Dungeon Break** | Failure state — monsters spill into real world if gate not cleared in time [^5^][^66^] | Referenced throughout |

#### The Demon Castle (Detailed)
- S-Rank Instant Dungeon created by Kandiaru, inhabited by Demons [^64^]
- **Appearance**: A destroyed version of Seoul — ruined buildings, on fire [^64^]
- Based on an alternate timeline where Rulers-Monarchs war devastated Earth [^64^]
- **Bosses**: Cerberus (Floor 1), Vulcan (Floor 50), Metus (Floor 75), Baran (Floor 100) [^64^]
- After clearing all 100 floors, the Castle was restored to its original state [^64^]

#### The Cartenon Temple / Double Dungeon (Detailed)
- Hidden inside a D-Rank gate — completely unexpected threat level [^131^]
- Contains no monsters, only **gigantic statues** designed by Kandiaru [^131^]
- Governed by **Three Commandments**: [^131^]
  1. Thou shall worship God (everyone must bow)
  2. Thou shall praise God (stand before instrument statues)
  3. Thou shall prove thy faith (one must remain as sacrifice)
- Violating any commandment = instant death by statue attack
- Third commandment requires one person to stay behind — Jinwoo volunteered [^131^]
- Surviving the trial = offered chance to become a Player of the System

### Visual Language & UI Patterns

- **Interface type**: Game-like, blue holographic screen [^124^][^128^]
- **Appearance**: Floating translucent screens reminiscent of PS Vita/game HUD [^146^]
- **HP bar**: Green bar at bottom left [^146^]
- **MP bar**: Blue bar at bottom left [^146^]
- **Notification style**: Bracketed text — `[Notification: You have unread messages.]` [^146^]
- **Pop-up windows**: Status, Quests, Weapons, Armor, Skills, Map, Inventory, Crafting tabs [^146^]
- **Time behavior**: Time appears to freeze when Jinwoo opens the System — he can review/reorganize without losing real-world time [^26^]
- **Visibility**: Only Jinwoo can see or interact with the interface [^26^]
- **Screen elements**: Quest checklists with progress `[0/100]`, check marks on completion [^104^]
- **Sound effects**: Game-like sounds accompany menu interactions [^146^]
- **Font style**: Clean, modern gaming-style text with bracketed headers
- **Quest formatting**: `[Skill: Shadow Extraction]`, `[Shadow Power. No mana required]` — structured data blocks [^65^]
- **Level-up notification**: Pop-up stating "New Level X! Gained 1 characteristic point" [^104^]
- **Color scheme**: Blue holographic primary, green for HP, red for warnings/damage, gold accents for rewards

### Key Phrases & Tone

| Phrase | Context |
|--------|---------|
| **"Arise"** | The command word for Shadow Extraction. THE most iconic phrase in the series. Jinwoo chose it himself. Activates the extraction of shadow soldiers from fallen enemies [^101^][^106^] |
| **"Congratulations on becoming a Player."** | The System's first message to Jinwoo after the Double Dungeon trial [^128^] |
| **"[Notification: Congratulations, you've become a Player!]"** | System welcome notification format [^146^] |
| **"The player has completed..."** | Standard quest completion announcement |
| **"Quest Complete"** | Success notification |
| **"You have failed the quest."** | Failure notification, triggers penalty |
| **"Penalty Quest Initiated"** | When daily quest is failed |
| **"Status Recovery"** | Full heal/replenish reward — cures all injuries, fatigue, poison |
| **"Will To Recover"** | Passive skill name — regenerates lost limbs |
| **"The System"** | Always referred to with reverence and capitalization |
| **"Player"** | Jinwoo's designation within the System — unique to him |
| **"Shadow Monarch"** | Jinwoo's ultimate class/title |
| **"It is not available"** | System response when trying to access locked content |

#### System Communication Tone
- **Mechanical but adaptive**: Follows programmed rules but adjusts to Jinwoo's behavior
- **Clinical**: Uses standardized, almost computer-like formatting for notifications
- **Non-judgmental**: Doesn't criticize failures, just assigns penalties
- **Helpful but hidden-agenda**: Every "helpful" feature serves the ultimate goal of preparing Ashborn's vessel [^124^]
- **Urgency levels**: 
  - Normal: Standard blue notifications
  - Warning: Red alerts for danger/murderous intent
  - Critical: Red urgent quests with death penalties
- **Life-protection mode**: Automatically activates when Jinwoo is in danger, triggers passive skills [^26^]
- **Hidden surveillance**: Monitors Jinwoo's surroundings for threats constantly [^23^]

### Shadow Extraction & Army Mechanics

#### Shadow Extraction Skill
- **Command word**: "Arise" (chosen by Jinwoo) [^101^][^106^]
- **Cost**: No mana required [^65^]
- **Effect**: Extracts mana from a lifeless body and turns it into a Shadow Soldier [^65^]
- **Failure rate**: Proportional to target's stats and time elapsed since death [^65^]
- **3 attempts max** per target [^65^]
- **Level 2 effect**: Shape Transformation — freely change appearance/shape of Shadow Soldiers [^65^]

#### Shadow Army Grade System (7 Tiers)
| Grade | Strength | Key Traits |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Normal** | E to C-Rank | Foot soldiers, no personality, no speech [^62^][^68^] |
| **Elite** | B-Rank | Most common grade, no speech [^62^][^68^] |
| **Knight** | A-Rank | Can be given a name by the Shadow Monarch [^62^][^68^] |
| **Elite Knight** | Basic S-Rank | Named, capable of complex combat [^62^] |
| **General/Commander** | Advanced S-Rank | Can speak, independent decision-making [^62^][^68^] |
| **Marshal/Commander** | Above S-Rank | Speaks, commands large army sections [^62^][^68^] |
| **Grand Marshal** | Unique | Commands entire shadow army; only **Bellion** holds this [^62^][^68^] |

#### Notable Named Shadow Soldiers
| Name | Origin | Grade |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Bellion** | Ashborn's original right-hand | Grand Marshal |
| **Beru** | Ant King of Jeju Island | Marshal |
| **Igris** | Blood-Red Commander of Job Change dungeon | Marshal |
| **Tusk** | High Orc Shaman Kargalgan | General |
| **Ant Queen** | Queen of Jeju Island ants | General |
| **Greed** | Hwang Dong-soo (S-Rank hunter) | General |
| **Iron** | Kim Chul (A-Rank hunter, died in Red Gate) | Elite Knight |
| **Kaisel** | Baran's wyvern (Demon Castle) | Elite Knight |

#### Shadow Army Abilities
- **Infinite regeneration**: Rebuild from being torn to pieces [^68^]
- **No fatigue**: No organs, fight indefinitely [^68^]
- **Full skill retention**: All abilities from original being carry over [^68^]
- **Absolute loyalty**: Serve without exception, extends to Shadow Monarch's bloodline [^68^]
- **Emotional awareness**: Higher grades sense master's emotional state [^68^]
- **Storage**: Soldiers stored in Jinwoo's shadow dimensional space, accessible from any shadow [^68^]

### Leveling Curve

| Level | XP to Reach | Total Skill Points |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 100 | 3 |
| 5 | 1,118 | 15 |
| 10 | 3,162 | 30 |
| 20 | 8,944 | 60 |
| 30 | 16,431 | 90 |
| 50 (Evolution) | 35,355 | 150 |
| 75 | 64,951 | 225 |
| 100 | 100,000 | 300 |

- Early levels are fast; curve steepens significantly after level 30 [^105^]
- Each level: all base stats +1, +5 skill points to distribute [^23^]
- Daily quest: +3 stat points per completion [^23^]
- No hard level cap in the broader system (though original System capped at 100) [^105^]
- Post-level 50: enemy attack scales up by 1.5% per level to maintain challenge [^105^]

### Store Mechanics (Detailed)

- **Currency**: Gold — earned from kills, quest completions, selling items [^23^]
- **Inventory scales to Player level**: Higher level = better items available [^23^]
- **Consumables**: Health potions, mana potions, stamina items [^23^]
- **Equipment**: Armor, accessories
- **Weapons**: Physical weapons like Knight Killer dagger [^23^]
- **Blessed Random Box**: Gives item the player *wants* [^23^]
- **Cursed Random Box**: Gives item the player *needs* (succession-driven) [^23^][^26^]
- **Purchase limits**: Daily purchase limits on consumables [^104^]
- **Notable items from Demon Castle**: Holy Water of Life (cures any disease, Class S) [^130^]

### Recommended Deep-Dive Areas for App Development

| Area | Why It Matters |
|------|----------------|
| **Daily Quest System** | Core engagement loop — physical training quests with penalty zones create urgency and habit formation. The 24-hour cycle with penalty creates natural FOMO |
| **Shadow Extraction Animation** | The "Arise" moment is THE most iconic visual in Solo Leveling. The shadow rising from a corpse, the command word, the naming of soldiers — all critical for app authenticity |
| **Stat Distribution UI** | The "+" icons, "Distribute Points" button, 5-point allocation system — core RPG loop that must feel exactly right |
| **Penalty Zone Design** | The dark desert, centipedes, survival timer — unique mechanic that differentiates from generic fitness apps. Creates consequence for missed goals |
| **Blue Holographic UI** | The visual identity of the System — translucent blue panels, bracketed notifications, HP/MP bars. Must be instantly recognizable |
| **Store & Loot Box Mechanics** | Two-box system (Blessed/Cursed) is unique and lore-accurate. Gold currency from "kills" (workouts) ties progression to action |
| **Job Change Quest Flow** | The progression from basic class → hidden class → ultimate class (Shadow Monarch) mirrors the user's own journey from beginner → advanced |
| **Shadow Army Management** | Grade system, naming soldiers, storage in shadow — rich social/guild-like features for future expansion |
| **Quest Types Variation** | Daily/Main/Urgent/Side quest structure provides content variety and narrative progression |
| **Level-Up Notification Style** | Bracketed text, clean data blocks, progress checklists — specific UI patterns that fans expect |
